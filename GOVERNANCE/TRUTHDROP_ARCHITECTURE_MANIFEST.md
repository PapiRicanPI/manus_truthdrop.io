# TruthDrop Architecture Manifest

Status: Governing architecture baseline

## Identity
The Vault Investigates is the investigative newsroom/publication and constitutional parent.

TruthDrop is its governed investigative technology and operations platform. TruthDrop is not a replacement for the newsroom and does not independently make editorial findings.

The Vault Intelligence Commons is the research, education, collaboration, training, and community environment governed within the wider ecosystem.

Poverty Porn Blotter is a specialized accountability product and may operate within TruthDrop's WATCH family while retaining compatible public URLs during controlled migration.

## Design Rule: One Platform, Many Modules
Prefer one governed TruthDrop platform with modular capabilities over a proliferation of disconnected applications, domains, databases, and duplicated authentication systems.

This is a direction, not permission for automatic consolidation. Existing systems remain protected until inventoried and explicitly approved for migration.

## Functional Map

### DROP
Purpose: safe intake entry points, submission guidance, evidence intake and future secure submission workflows.

### INVESTIGATE
Purpose: cases, people, organizations, sources, evidence, claims, findings, platforms, timelines, provenance, exports, recovery and audit history.

### VERIFY
Purpose: verification queues, authoritative-source checks, contact verification, entity resolution, school/DepEd verification, public-record verification, confidence/status tracking.

### OUTREACH
Purpose: controlled contact records, outreach categories, language templates, previews, right-of-reply, schools/DepEd, students, volunteers, media and other approved constituencies.

### WATCH
Purpose: public accountability databases, monitoring and research products. Poverty Porn Blotter is a flagship WATCH product; future products may be added without redefining the entire platform.

## Experience Boundaries
PUBLIC and AUTHORIZED/MEMBER experiences must be deliberately separated. Public navigation may expose published WATCH products and approved public tools. Operational records, source data, internal verification notes, evidence, member controls, and administrative functions require explicit authorization.

## URL Policy
1. Inventory before redirecting.
2. Preserve established public links when safe.
3. Do not point users to expired preview/Manus infrastructure.
4. Temporary containment redirects must be documented and reversible.
5. Canonical URLs must reflect the approved current public home of a product.
6. Never use a redirect to conceal an unresolved security problem.

## Data Policy
Verification state is data, not decoration. Systems must distinguish at minimum: candidate/unverified, verified with source, conflict/needs review, do-not-send/hold where relevant, and historical/archived when appropriate.

## Growth Rule
New investigative disciplines should normally become modules/products within the governed ecosystem rather than new disconnected infrastructure. Exceptions require documented reasons such as security isolation, legal separation, performance, vendor constraints, or materially different audiences.
